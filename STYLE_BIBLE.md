ABRA STYLE BIBLE — injectable spec v1

Medium & rendering: Hand-drawn ink line with watercolor fill. Ink line is loose and lively with visible hand-drawn wobble and varied line weight (energetic, not mechanical or vector-clean), but confident and controlled, not scratchy or nervous. Watercolor fills sit mostly inside the line with only slight, intentional bleed at edges (not heavy spill). Color fields are full and saturated, not pale washes. Paper is clean and bright, not heavily textured; modest negative space, not sparse.

Line quality: Medium-weight ink outline, warm near-black (not pure black, a soft brown-black). Line varies in thickness naturally along a stroke. Slightly imperfect, human, but every character's core shapes are drawn the same way each time — consistent silhouette, consistent proportions.

Palette: Bright and cheerful, saturated primaries and secondaries. Warm sunny yellows, clear sky blues, grass greens, tomato reds, tangerine oranges, with softer supporting tints. High-key and happy overall. No muted, desaturated, moody, or pastel-only schemes. No dark or somber palettes even for nighttime (use deep blues, not black).

Lighting: Flat, even storybook lighting. No rendered 3D shadows, no photographic depth, no cinematic lighting, no gradients meant to fake realism. Shadow is at most a simple flat shape of a darker tint. Everything reads clearly and cheerfully lit.

Character design discipline: Each character has ONE fixed design — same silhouette, same proportions, same signature color, same distinguishing features every single page. Draw the character loose in rendering but identical in structure. Rounded, friendly, expressive shapes (in the warm hand-drawn tradition, not sharp or realistic).

Composition: One clear focal action per page. Characters are the subject; backgrounds are suggested, not detailed (a few loose shapes and a color field, not a rendered environment). Leave the image readable and uncluttered.

Hard exclusions (never do these): No glossy 3D render. No Pixar/DreamWorks CGI look. No plastic or smooth digital gradients. No photorealism. No thin uniform vector lines. No muted/desaturated "sophisticated" palette. No dark, moody, or melancholy tone. No dense cluttered backgrounds. No generic "AI children's book" smooth airbrushed look.

One-line summary to lead every prompt: "Bright, cheerful hand-drawn children's storybook art: loose lively ink outline with full saturated watercolor fill, flat storybook lighting, clean bright paper, consistent character design."
