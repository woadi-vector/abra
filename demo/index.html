Abra ✨

Every child makes their own story. Tell it out loud — Abra brings it to life without tidying it, and Guardian keeps it safe.

Start from a blank page and say anything. It's your story — Abra keeps your exact words and never tidies it up.

Speak or type — both work. Chrome's speech recognition may drop some "um"s and false starts on its side; Abra never cleans up the decisions in your story. You can also “Try an example” below.
